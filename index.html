Quality

White, fluffy and never sticky.

That's what our customers ask rice to be. So that's what we check for, batch after batch.

Paddy checked at the gate

Good rice can't be made from poor paddy. We reject it before it enters the mill.

Rated ZED Gold

The Government of India's highest MSME rating for quality and zero waste, earned in 2024.

FSSAI licensed

Every sack is milled and packed under a food safety licence.

Before a sack is stitched, we check

  • That the grains are clean: no stones, husk or dust
  • That colour and grain size match the last batch
  • That it cooks white and fluffy, and stays non-sticky for hours
  • That every sack weighs what it says

Paddy to pack

How paddy becomes Kilimark rice.

We buy only paddy that passes our check. It is cleaned, parboiled and dried, then milled, sorted and packed by machine. The same steps and the same settings, every day.

Our story

He sold rice for years before he milled any.

P. T. Janakiraman started behind the counter of a rice shop in Thathaneri, listening to what families expected from every sack. In 1998 he began milling to deliver exactly that.

  1. 1996
    PTJ Rice Mill Depot opensA rice shop in Thathaneri, Madurai. Still open today.
  2. 1998
    Kilimark beginsFrom trader to miller, with no milling legacy to inherit.
  3. 2012
    The mill goes automaticMachines take over the heavy manual work, and every batch comes out the same.
  4. 2018
    Supplying beyond Tamil NaduKilimark reaches buyers across state borders.
  5. 2024
    Rated ZED GoldIndia's highest MSME rating for quality and zero waste.

Source carefully. Process responsibly. Deliver consistently.

How we've worked since 1998

Today he runs the mill with his son, Himalesh, who joined after his MBA to take Kilimark beyond Madurai.

Why a parrot

A parrot never changes its word.

In Madurai, the green parrot in Goddess Meenakshi's hand is said to carry devotees' prayers to her, word for word. That parrot is on every Kilimark sack. It stands for three things we will not change.
